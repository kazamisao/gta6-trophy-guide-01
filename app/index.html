    Sources & honesty

    Every trophy's name, description and grade in this guide is shown as PlayStation publishes it, from the official list as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it — the two agree on every name, description and grade. Counts such as 250 stunts or 42 Shooting Range challenges come from those same lists.

    Each guide is written in our own words for this entry and cites its sources beside it. Where a guide names a place, a count or a step, that fact comes from the official description or from the two list sources. The game released on 19 November 2026 and no complete verified walkthrough exists yet, so where no source has verified a detail — a cryptic description, a trigger nobody has confirmed — the guide says so plainly instead of inventing it. A cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed; an honest “unverified” beats a confident guess.

    Missable flags are caution flags built from the official descriptions, not confirmed facts: a trophy tied to a story chapter, a story mission or a stranger strand is flagged “possibly missable” because the descriptions do not say those missions or strands can be replayed. Nothing in the list is flagged missable on evidence beyond the descriptions.

    There are no online trophies in the official list: all 55 are single-player as described. The “Online” filter therefore shows none, by design.

    This guide works fully offline. It fetches nothing at run time, sets no cookie and registers no service worker. Your checklist lives only in this browser's localStorage until you export it.

    An original guide. No Rockstar Games logo, official artwork, box art or game screenshot is used anywhere in it.
